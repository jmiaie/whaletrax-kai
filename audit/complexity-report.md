## 2. Complexity & Cohesion Assessment

### `main.py` (437 lines)
- **Functions:** ~10-12 CLI commands/groups.
- **Complexity:** High cyclomatic complexity in individual command functions due to progress bar handling, API calls, and logic mixed with display calls.
- **Cohesion Violations:** Mixes CLI definition with business logic and orchestration.
- **Boundaries:** The `wallethound` sub-commands and general `scan-*` commands could be grouped or abstracted into an orchestration layer.

### `src/wallet_scanner.py` (287 lines)
- **Functions:** 10 functions.
- **Complexity:** High branching in `_parse_trade`, `_parse_position`, and `_enrich_stats_from_closed_positions` (multiple `.get()` fallbacks).
- **Cohesion Violations:** Mixes raw API response parsing (Data Transformation) with statistical aggregation (Business Logic).
- **Boundaries:** Parsing logic for raw Polymarket objects should be moved to a dedicated parser or into the `models.py` as factory methods.

### `src/display.py` (204 lines)
- **Cohesion:** Mostly high, but contains private formatting helpers that are duplicated or similar in other display modules.

## 3. Recommended Refactoring

1.  **Extract Parsers from `src/wallet_scanner.py`**: Move `_parse_*` functions to `src/parsers.py` to leave `wallet_scanner.py` focused on the scanning workflow.
2.  **Lean `main.py`**: Move heavy command logic into a `src/commands/` or similar service layer. Currently, the `Progress` context managers are repeated and bulky.
3.  **Consolidate Display Helpers**: `_fmt_usdc`, `_fmt_pct`, etc., are likely needed by both `src/display.py` and `src/wallethound/display.py`. Move to `src/utils/formatting.py`.
